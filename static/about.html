What is Mercury?

Democratizing data science through intent-driven AI.

Our Mission

Mercury DataCleaner bridges the gap between raw data and actionable insights. By translating plain English into deterministic data operations, we eliminate the need for complex pandas boilerplate and expensive data engineering pipelines. We believe cleaning data should happen at the speed of thought.
