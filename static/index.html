Frequently Asked Questions

Is my data secure?
Yes. Mercury processes all dataset transformations locally. We do not upload your CSV files to any external AI models for training, and everything remains on your local server.

Yes! Because we utilize local SQLite architecture instead of heavy cloud databases, we are able to provide this tool at absolutely zero cost to you.

Not at all. You can just type "remove all rows where age is negative" and our deterministic intent engine handles the exact Pandas logic for you safely.
